# Automotive launch boundary

PeekaListing Automotive launches as a cars-first marketplace for Zimbabwe while preserving the reusable marketplace platform underneath.

## Public launch surface

- Cars are the only public asset discovery category.
- Vehicle sellers can submit cars directly after authentication.
- New vehicle submissions remain `pending_review` until the existing moderation flow approves publication.
- Approved car dealerships remain distinguishable through business-profile verification; dealership approval is not required merely to submit a private vehicle.
- Automotive services remain curated business inventory.
- Public Peeks and Peek Requests are retained and use vehicle-specific evidence prompts.

## Vehicle discovery

The launch search supports independent vehicle facets including make, vehicle category, condition, fuel type, transmission, body type, drivetrain, import status, model-year range, maximum mileage, price/currency, location and current-Peek availability.

## Dormant platform capacity

Property, machinery and the wider service/category architecture remain in the repository and database for future expansion, but they are not exposed as primary public launch navigation or discovery surfaces.

## Trust boundary

Opening car submission does not make listings self-publishing. The database publisher gate permits an active authenticated owner to create a car submission, while the listing creation transaction continues to place it into moderation before it is visible publicly. Identity spoofing and non-car publishing remain blocked by the existing curated rules.
